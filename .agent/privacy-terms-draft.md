# Privacy and service terms — internal draft for review

**Not published, not approved legal terms.** Operator identity, jurisdiction,
contact details, applicable rights, retention periods and provider agreements
must be supplied and reviewed before launch. No compliance certification is claimed.

## Privacy wording draft

Momentum AI stores your workspace records, conversations, learning/interview
practice, notification settings and memories you propose. Memories must be approved
before they are used as long-term context; editing an approved memory requires
approval again. Conversation history and approved memory are separate.

Neon services provide authentication and database storage. Gemini receives the
messages and selected context needed for an AI request. Provider processing and
retention details must be confirmed in the operator's final policy. External
opportunity links lead to their source websites and are governed by those sites.

Browser push is optional. Enabling it stores subscription credentials privately;
notifications use generic text. Scheduled delivery depends on worker availability,
device support, permissions and external push services.

You can export application workspace data and delete supported individual records.
Exports exclude sign-in credentials and push secrets. Full account erasure,
backup deletion and audit retention procedures remain unfinished; the final policy
must clearly describe the actual controls and request process before launch.

## Service wording draft

Momentum AI helps organize work and provides AI-generated suggestions and coaching.
AI responses may be inaccurate. Coaching scores are learning feedback, not proof
of mastery, qualifications or predictions of hiring outcomes. Verify opportunity
eligibility, availability and deadlines on the source website.

Proposed workspace actions require your approval. Review the details before
approving. Delivery of reminders and availability of external services cannot be
guaranteed. Do not rely on Momentum for emergency or time-critical safety needs.

The operator must define acceptable use, account eligibility, suspension and
termination terms, support responsibilities, service changes, dispute handling,
and any future pricing before publishing binding terms. No paid subscription,
uptime guarantee or refund policy is currently represented by this draft.
